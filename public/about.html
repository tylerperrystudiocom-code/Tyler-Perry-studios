The Legacy of Tyler Perry Studios

From a historic military base to the largest film production studio in the United States.

Tyler Perry Studios (TPS) is an American film production studio in Atlanta, Georgia, founded by actor, filmmaker, and playwright Tyler Perry in 2006. What began as a vision to create a space for underrepresented stories has grown into one of the largest film production studios in the United States, establishing Perry as the first African American to outright own a major film production studio.

The Historic Fort McPherson

In June 2015, Perry purchased 330 acres of the former Fort McPherson military complex for $30 million, investing over $250 million into its transformation. The site itself has a storied legacy dating back to the 19th century, having served as a Confederate Army base during the American Civil War and later as a U.S. Army post.

The acquisition turned a former symbol of military history into a beacon of modern creativity, preserving 40 historic buildings recognized by the National Register of Historic Places.

A New Era of Production

The grand opening in October 2019 marked a milestone for Black-owned studios. The campus features 12 purpose-built sound stages (the largest at 60,000 square feet), a 75,000-square-foot water tank, 200 acres of greenspace, and diverse backlots.

The soundstages are named after entertainment trailblazers like Oprah Winfrey, Spike Lee, Sidney Poitier, and Denzel Washington, honoring those who paved the way. The studio has hosted major productions including Marvel's Black Panther, Coming 2 America, and Perry's own hit series Sistas and The Oval.
